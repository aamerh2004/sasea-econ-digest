Pakistan Brief

SBP forex reserves rose $1.2bn to $18.33bn in the week to Sept. 4, and remittances climbed 16.5% y/y to $3.66bn in August; the rupee held near 277.3/USD, with Fitch Solutions (BMI) pushing back its depreciation forecast to 2027 and revising end-2026 to Rs278/USD from Rs288. An IMF mission is due September 23 for the biannual review of the $7bn Extended Fund Facility and the Resilience and Sustainability Facility. Offsetting this, weekly inflation (SPI) rose 8.62% y/y on onion and fuel prices, the PSX fell for a fourth straight session before a mild recovery, and petrol/diesel prices were hiked for a fifth consecutive review (petrol to Rs375.82/litre) as regional oil-supply disruptions from the Red Sea and Strait of Hormuz push costs higher. Islamabad also approved a further $200m ADB digitalization loan despite Planning Commission objections, and FBR told a Senate panel it is retaining Rs390bn in refunds under IMF-linked conditions.
